Himalayan Cacao is a premium tree-to-bar chocolate brand from Nepal. Each batch begins with cacao and ends in small-batch chocolate made with restraint, clarity, and a deep respect for origin.

The collection is focused and natural: refined chocolate bars and small-batch couverture for makers, chefs, bakeries, restaurants, hotels, and pastry kitchens.

From the Arun Valley

Rooted in eastern Nepal.

Himalayan Cacao begins in the Arun Valley of Sankhuwasabha, eastern Nepal — one of the world's deepest valleys. Here, the valley floor sits low and lush between two of the world's highest peaks, Mt. Makalu and Mt. Everest.

It is a landscape of extraordinary contrast: tropical soil, mountain air, and centuries of local farming knowledge.

We're partnering with a farm here to explore what Nepali cacao can become — rooted in Himalayan soil, nurtured by local hands, and shaped into chocolate that carries the taste and story of this place. From the Himalayas, with cacao.
